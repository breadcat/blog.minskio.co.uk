title: Medical MDMA
dream: dated
date: 2026-06-11
---

I was driving my new car through town when I was approaching a T junction.

I was looking pretty much through the car in front to the junction ahead and managed to bump into the car in front at low speed.

We both got out and the driver was someone I knew from school but they didn't recognise who I was.

I was apologetic and professional asking to trade insurance details but they were frantic and blabbering about how she was on 'medical MDMA' but insisting that her doctor couldn't find out about it.

I walked around both cars and there was no damage to either but she was following me still telling me I couldn't speak to her doctor.
