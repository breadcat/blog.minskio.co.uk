This dream is in the style of a music video from the 90's.

There are shots interspersed of the singer of the band, a tall, pale white guy with long blonde hair singing in the style of System of a Down. In some shots, he's riding alone on a roller coaster travelling on a track through a CGI mountain with waterfalls in the background. In others, he's singing riding in a silver hot air balloon on his own.

The other shots of the music video are of an older woman doing various cleaning jobs in a dark and dingy city where you'll look up to a building, see her cleaning windows, then look down and see the same woman sweeping the street with a broom all while maintaining eye contact with you and singing along.

The lyrics of the song are meaningful sounding gibberish like "You have consumed the path of knowledge".
